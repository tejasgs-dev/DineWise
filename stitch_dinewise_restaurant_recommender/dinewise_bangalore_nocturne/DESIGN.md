## Brand & Style
The design system delivers an upscale, late-evening culinary concierge interface tailored to Bangalore’s vibrant food, microbrewery, and cafe culture. The aesthetic pairs the intimacy of a dimly lit high-end dining lounge with the sharp precision of a state-of-the-art computational engine.

- **Brand Personality**: Discerning, intelligent, kinetic, and deeply sensory. It rejects the hyper-stimulating, utilitarian discount banners common in delivery aggregators, opting instead for curatorial elegance and effortless discovery.
- **Visual Style**: Nocturnal Minimalist Luxe accented by ambient chromatic halos. The deep charcoal canvas prioritizes food photography and dynamic recommendation models, anchoring attention through warm culinary saffron light and soft violet algorithmic intuition.
- **Emotional Intent**: Evokes confidence, appetite, sophistication, and effortless command—transforming chaotic restaurant discovery across Indiranagar, Koramangala, and Lavelle Road into a bespoke, curated journey.

## Colors
The palette is built on strict charcoal tonal tiers to establish natural spatial depth without introducing visual glare in night environments.

- **Canvas & Surfaces**:
  - `canvas-default`: `#0F0F12` (Base ground)
  - `surface-card`: `#18181D` (Standard cards, list rows, bottom sheets)
  - `surface-raised`: `#222228` (Hover states, elevated popovers, active dialogs)
  - `border-subtle`: `#2A2A31` (Structural 1px outlines)
  - `border-focus`: `rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.4)`
- **Brand Accents**:
  - **Saffron Orange (`#FF7A1A`)**: Primary CTA elements, active tags, review stars, and high-affinity culinary triggers.
  - **Soft Violet (`#9B7BFF`)**: Dedicated exclusively to generative AI capabilities, contextual recommendation sparks, match percentages, and conversational agent bubbles.
- **Semantic Accents**:
  - **Status Amber (`#F5B544`)**: Trending badges, mid-tier price indicators, and wait-time notifications.
  - **Emerald Green (`#3DD68C`)**: Dietary pure-veg indicators, instant table availability, and 90%+ match tiers.
  - **Crimson Red (`#FF5C5C`)**: Fast-filling reservation alerts, non-veg markers, and destructive flows.
- **Text & Content**:
  - `text-primary`: `#FFFFFF` (100% opacity, crisp contrast against `#0F0F12`)
  - `text-secondary`: `#A0A0AB` (Secondary descriptions, metadata)
  - `text-tertiary`: `#666672` (Disabled states, fine print, placeholder values)

## Typography
The system leverages `Plus Jakarta Sans` for display and heading roles to infuse a warm, contemporary, welcoming editorial tone into culinary titles and venue branding. `Inter` serves all body, interactive labels, and tabular metadata to maintain crisp, utilitarian clarity across high-density listings.

- **Tabular Figures**: All currency values (`₹`), distances, ratings, time stamps, and match percentages must use `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` to prevent layout jitter during dynamic queries or real-time filtering.
- **Editorial Contrast**: Pair `display-hero` with subtle `text-secondary` subheaders to maintain breathable hierarchy above dense restaurant grids.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 4-column layout on mobile scales to 8 columns on tablet and 12 columns on desktop. Spacing anchors around a strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm designed to accommodate multi-attribute discovery cards (photos, tags, AI insights, price markers) without crowding.

- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Bottom navigation is pinned with safe-area padding. Restaurant cards occupy full column widths.
- **Tablet (768px–1024px)**: 8-column layout, `margin-md: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Cards arrange into 2-column grids with horizontal metadata orientation.
- **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12-column layout, max content boundary `1280px`, `margin-lg: 2rem`, `gutter-lg: 1.5rem`. Split architecture: sticky AI interactive prompt and map on the left/right, fluid list feeds spanning remaining columns.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation eschews conventional muddy drop shadows in favor of a layered tonal system accented by crisp micro-borders and directional luminosity.

- **Stacking Tiers**:
  - `Layer 0 (Canvas)`: `#0F0F12` with no shadow.
  - `Layer 1 (Card Base)`: `#18181D` bounded by a 1px solid `#2A2A31` perimeter.
  - `Layer 2 (Raised Popovers & Dropdowns)`: `#222228` with an ultra-diffused ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
  - `Layer 3 (Modal / Overlays)`: `#222228` flanked by `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)` and a top-edge highlight stroke `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Atmospheric Glows**:
  - **AI Match Luminescence**: Recommendation cards and AI prompt bars feature a soft radial violet underglow: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(155, 123, 255, 0.15)`.
  - **Primary Action Bloom**: Primary buttons on hover produce a tight saffron flare: `0 4px 16px rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.3)`.

## Shapes
The shape hierarchy employs structured geometric corners for major bounding viewports and content containers, transitioning to organic full-pill geometry for metadata badges, actionable chips, and interactive buttons.

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px)**: Text input boxes, dropdown menus, context submenus.
- **Container Surfaces (1rem / 16px)**: Restaurant presentation cards, dietary modal drawers, curated collection tiles.
- **Hero Containers (1.5rem / 24px)**: Spotlight recommendation banners and AI chat interaction panels.
- **Pill Shapes (Full / 9999px)**: Filter chips, cuisine selection pills, match percentage tags, quick prompts, and action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Saffron orange background (`#FF7A1A`), dark label (`#0F0F12`), `label-lg`, pill-shaped radius. Active state: `#E5660B`. Glow: `0 4px 16px rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.25)`.
- **AI Assist Action**: Solid `#9B7BFF` or subtle translucent fill (`rgba(155, 123, 255, 0.12)`), violet label (`#9B7BFF`), 1px border (`rgba(155, 123, 255, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Background `#222228`, border 1px `#2A2A31`, white label (`#FFFFFF`). Hover: border `#FF7A1A`.

### Filter & Cuisine Chips
- **Default State**: Surface `#18181D`, border 1px `#2A2A31`, text `#A0A0AB`, pill radius, `space-xs` vertical by `space-md` horizontal padding.
- **Selected State**: Background `rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.15)`, border 1px solid `#FF7A1A`, text `#FF7A1A`.
- **AI Filter Indicator**: Soft violet dot (`#9B7BFF`) prefixed to text to denote hyper-personalized dynamic filters (e.g., "Quiet Cafes with WiFi in Indiranagar").

### Restaurant & Recommendation Cards
- **Base Structure**: `#18181D` surface with 1px `#2A2A31` outline and `16px` border-radius.
- **Media Header**: Aspect-ratio 16:9 image overlayed with a subtle bottom-to-top gradient (`rgba(24, 24, 29, 0.8)` to transparent) carrying the AI match badge on the top right (`98% Match` in emerald green `#3DD68C` over `#0F0F12`).
- **Card Body**: Restaurant name in `headline-sm`, followed by cuisine and micro-neighborhood (e.g., "Artisanal Sourdough & Craft Roasts • Koramangala 4th Block") in `body-sm` (`#A0A0AB`).
- **Footer**: Tabular pricing (`₹₹₹`) and distance metadata aligned opposite an instant reservation button.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Container**: Background `#18181D`, border 1px solid `#2A2A31`, height `48px`, radius `8px`, typography `body-md`.
- **Focus**: Border `#FF7A1A` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.15)`).
- **AI Concierge Prompt Bar**: Taller input (`56px`), `#222228` surface, 1px violet-tinted border (`rgba(155, 123, 255, 0.3)`), with an active sparkling glyph prefix.

### Lists & Menu Item Rows
- **Layout**: Separated by 1px subtle divider lines (`#2A2A31`), omitting outer bounds for clean horizontal breathing space.
- **Dietary Identifiers**: Square icons with rounded 2px corners—Green dot enclosed for Pure Veg, Brown triangle for Non-Veg.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Frame**: 20px box or circular ring, border 2px solid `#666672`, background `#18181D`.
- **Checked State**: Background `#FF7A1A`, border `#FF7A1A`, containing an internal white checkmark or center pip.
